**Divergences section** — explicitly call out the three most narrative-rich divergences

1. **Operator mutation vs semantic patch (add_business_days):** mutant's M-AB-25 inverts the
   direction flag with a mechanical operator swap (`positive?` → `negative?`). LLM's LM-AB-01
   adds a realistic weekend-normalizer a developer might actually write. Both survive, but only
   the LLM mutation could fool a code reviewer — it looks like a legitimate defensive improvement.

2. **Catastrophic removal vs targeted fix (weeks_between):** mutant's M-WB-01 removes the `/ 7`
   entirely — a mutation that should be killed by any test checking a non-zero result. Its
   survival exposes a deeper problem: some mutant mutations report alive due to Ruby `module_function`

   reasons.

   no test checks year 2000. Coverage metrics and mutant alike are blind to whether your test
   suite validates the complete Gregorian calendar rule.
